Smart Lab Siena LoRa — brand assets

Concept: a simplified Siena tower as the center of a long-range mesh network.
The warm terracotta references Siena's brick architecture; teal nodes and radio arcs suggest connectivity.

Files in this folder
- smartlab-lora-logo.svg: horizontal wordmark for web, documentation, and app materials.
- smartlab-lora-app-icon.svg: editable square icon concept, 512 × 512 viewBox.
- smartlab-lora-app-icon.png: square raster preview/app icon.
- android-ic-launcher-foreground.xml and android-ic-launcher-background.xml: source copies of the adaptive-icon layers installed at androidApp/src/main/res/drawable-anydpi/.
